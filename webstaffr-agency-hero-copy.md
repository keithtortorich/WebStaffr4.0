## Pricing FAQ

**Why is the website free?**
Because we would rather show you what WebStaffr can do than tell you. Once you see
it working, the rest is an easy decision.

**What happens after 14 days?**
You choose whether to keep your website and staff by subscribing, or step away with
no obligation. Either way, no surprise charges.

**Can I switch plans later?**
Yes. Most contractors start with Office Staff and move to Business Manager once they
have seen the results.

**Is there a setup fee?**
No. What you see is what you pay.
