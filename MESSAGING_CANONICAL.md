## TONE RULES

1. **Speak to the pain, not the feature** — "You lose jobs to competitors who answered first" beats "Our AI receptionist handles calls 24/7"
2. **Use contractor language** — "on a job," "on a ladder," "on the road" — not "mobile workforce"
3. **Make the math visceral** — "$500–$5,000 per missed job" beats "improved conversion rates"
4. **Never say "AI"** — Say "receptionist," "staff," "team," "your 24/7 answerer"
5. **Assume the reader is exhausted** — Short sentences. Specific numbers. No jargon.
