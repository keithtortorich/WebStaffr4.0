# Referral Program

Status: approved by the founder as the program's own source of truth (new program, no prior governance to reconcile against). One open arithmetic item below still needs a call before this goes live with real dollar amounts attached.

## How Payouts Work

Base reward is the standard payout before multipliers. Tier and Loyalty multipliers stack (Tier x Loyalty).

Example: Gold (2x) + 12-month Loyalty (2x) on a $25 base = $100 upfront.

## Track 1: Affiliate (open to anyone)

| Reward | Amount |
|---|---|
| Upfront (base) | $25 x multipliers |
| Recurring | $15/month for 12 months |
| Retention | $50 at 6 months, $100 at 12 months |
| Max per referral | See open item below |

## Track 2: Client Referral (existing customers)

- Referrer chooses one: $30 bill credit, a free $197 add-on month, or $30 account credit.
- Mutual: both referrer and the new client get $20 credit at the 6-month and 12-month marks.
- Long-term clients get extra retention credits (amount not yet specified).

## Advocate Tiers (cumulative, both tracks)

| Tier | Referrals | Multiplier | Perks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 3 | 1.5x | Badge, priority support |
| Silver | 8 | 1.75x | Extra support |
| Gold | 15+ | 2x | Recognition, co-marketing, swag |

## Loyalty Multiplier

| Active | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 6+ months | 1.5x |
| 12+ months | 2x |

Resets to 1x after 6 consecutive months with no referrals.

## Logistics

- Tracking: unique referral link/code issued on enrollment.
- Success metric: first payment from the new client.
- Payouts: monthly, batched, $50 minimum.
- Credits expire 12 months after issue.
- Rules: no self-referrals; referrer must have an active subscription.
- Contact: referrals@WebStaffr.agency
